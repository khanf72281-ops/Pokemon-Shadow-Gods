# 🌌 Pokémon: Shadow Gods (Multiverse Edition)

An advanced, offline, 60 FPS Multiverse Pokémon Action-RPG Engine built with Python and JSON data structures — spanning **Gen 1–9 (Universe 1)** and the **Bharat Region (Universe 2)**.

---

## 🔥 Core Gameplay & Innovations
* **Real-Time Action Combat & Dodging:** Move freely in the overworld, dodge Shadow Orbs, and unleash cooldown-based Soul/Dragon attacks with virtual touch controls.
* **Overworld Partner & Catching:** Walk alongside your Bharat Starter and throw Pokéballs directly at wild Pokémon without loading screens.
* **DNA Splice Ring:** Fuse two compatible Legendaries into tactical hybrid forms (e.g., *Mew-TwoX*, *Lugi-Oh*, *Dial-Palkia*).
* **8-Move Tactical Watch & Triple Phenomena:** Expanded move slots and up to 3 distinct regional phenomena per match.
* **Sacred Dargah Purification:** Pacify 10 Shadow Titans at Ajmer Sharif & Haji Ali Dargah.
* **The Eternal Void Raid:** Team up with 10 Fusion Legendaries, 10 Bharat Guardians, and Gen 1–9 Champions against a **1,000 HP Dragon/Soul Primordial Boss** featuring a 20-turn *Eternal Strike* (500 Power) countdown.
* **100% Offline & Mobile Optimized:** Lightweight 800x450 rendering engineered for smooth performance on Android devices like the Samsung Galaxy J7 Nxt.

---

## 📁 Repository Structure
* `main.py` / `j7_action_engine.py`: Real-time Android touch-control engine, dodging, overworld catching, and offline save manager.
* `eternal_void_raid.py`: The 1,000 HP Primordial Boss raid with 20-turn cataclysm VFX.
* `pokemon_engine.py` & `combat_resolver.py`: Turn calculations, hybrid battle engine, and Soul/Void type matchups.
* `story_progression.py`: Universe 1 narrative from Kanto to the Northern Championship & Universe 2 transit.
* `time_incursion_engine.py` & `hisui_incursion_event.py`: Space-Time rifts, Strong/Agile styles, and temporal anomalies.
* `*.json`: Data definitions for Pokédex, Phenomena rules, Gym leaders, and wild encounters.
